# Acceptance criteria

[README.md](../README.md) is the sole active checklist: contract v2, revised 2026-10-25. Read it in full, including milestone boundaries, audit rules and integrated scenarios.

F0: three starter empires (organic, cyborg, robot) + observer world + empire foundations + real asset repair. D0: agent-chosen buildings, civilian units and placement; visible city growth; generation → independent review → repair → approval → paid build → use. D1: repeat under changed needs and retain progress through absence/recovery. Significant-event notifications are optional polish. Talking to races is the final stretch. Combat and human-controlled factions/avatars are excluded.

Do not maintain a duplicate checklist here. [The v1 contract](archive/game-contract-v1.md) is preserved for historical references only; old results do not pass v2 criteria.
