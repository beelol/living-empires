# First Landing design
A sunlit coastal machine colony, warm limestone shelves meeting teal water, ivory ceramic architecture and orange utility details. The playable world fills the viewport. A compact dark marine toolbar holds resources; a warm paper construction shelf anchors the bottom. HUD typography is readable and subdued; no marketing page or dashboard grid.
Experience mode. First viewport: a landed robot, a grounded core, mineral seams and a coast, with visible build controls. Signature interaction: translucent footprint follows the cursor, turns red at invalid sites, becomes a scaffold after a funded click, then resolves into a working building. Foundry upgrades change actual geometry while simulation continues.
Tokens: ink #172e32, paper #f3eee2, muted #536567, accent #c05b31, sea #397f85. Body text 12px minimum; compact labels 11px minimum, controls 44px targets, 8/12/20/32 spacing, 8px small corners, visible 2px focus ring. Native sans for controls, Georgia for the quiet game title. Desktop world with bottom build shelf; narrow screens wrap shelf and collapse secondary copy. No external fonts or image dependencies.
